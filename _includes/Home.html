Hello, I'm Chan Tsz Ying 你好，我是 Chan Tsz Ying

Welcome to my personal corner of the internet where I share my academic research, film photography, and encounters with urban wildlife. My passion for nature and environmental issues was sparked by a memorable freshwater and amphibian eco-tour. This experience left a lasting impression on me, leading me to pursue a Bachelor's degree in Environmental Management at HKBU, a Master's degree in Environmental Management at HKU, and eventually begin my MPhil studies. 歡迎來到我的個人網站，我會在這裡分享我的學術研究、底片攝影，以及與城市野生動物的相遇。我對自然與環境議題的熱情，源自於一次難忘的淡水與兩棲動物生態導覽。那次經驗令我印象深刻，並啟發我先後在香港浸會大學（HKBU）完成環境管理學士學位、在香港大學（HKU）取得環境管理碩士學位，最終展開我的哲學碩士（MPhil）研究旅程。

My Academic Path 我的學術旅程

I am an MPhil student at Lingnan University in Hong Kong. Currently, my research focuses on urban bee and wasp communities in Hong Kong, using a functional trait-based approach to assess how floral resources and microclimate affect community assembly across the urban gradient. To see a full list of my published papers and current research projects, please visit my academic portfolio. 我目前是香港嶺南大學的哲學碩士生。我的研究主要關注香港城市的蜜蜂與黃蜂群落，利用基於功能性狀的方法，評估花卉資源與微氣候如何影響城市化梯度中的群落組成。如欲查看我發表的論文與當前研究項目的完整清單，請瀏覽我的學術頁面。
